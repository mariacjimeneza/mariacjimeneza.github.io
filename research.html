Publications

  • Evolution of Returns to Education in Colombia (1976–2014)

    with Jaime Tenjo, Oriana Álvarez and Alejandro Gaviria · Coyuntura Económica, 47(1–2), 2017, pp. 15–48.

    Abstract

    We estimate returns to education in Colombia from household surveys over nearly four decades, comparing several econometric specifications and correcting for possible selection bias. Returns to pre-university education fell steadily over the period, while returns to post-secondary education levelled off at around 20% from 1995 onwards.

    Journal page RePEc

  • Diferencias en las tasas de desempleo por género

    with Jaime Tenjo and Oriana Álvarez · In L. E. Arango, F. Castellani and E. Lora (eds.), Desempleo femenino en Colombia, ch. 3, pp. 67–99. Bogotá: Banco de la República and Inter-American Development Bank, 2016.

    Abstract

    The chapter documents how gender differences in unemployment have evolved in Colombia, reviews the theoretical arguments and the Colombian literature, and analyses the gap empirically. Women experience longer unemployment spells than men, especially when married and with children, and stronger local economic activity shortens unemployment spells considerably more for women than for men.
