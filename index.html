About

I am a PhD candidate in Regional Science and Economic Geography at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI), supervised by Daria Denti and Alessandra Faggian. I am an applied economist working on labour and regional economics, with a focus on gender.

My research studies how public policies affect women and men differently across places, including welfare cuts, transport investment and family policy. My paper on austerity and sexual violence reporting in England and Wales is forthcoming in the Journal of Economic Geography.
