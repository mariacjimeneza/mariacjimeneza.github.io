About

I am a PhD candidate in Regional Science and Economic Geography at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI) in L’Aquila, Italy. My research sits at the intersection of labor and regional economics: I study gender gaps in labor market outcomes and how they vary across places, using household surveys and applied econometric methods. Before the PhD, I worked as a research assistant at Universidad Jorge Tadeo Lozano in Bogotá on projects on returns to education, gender differences in unemployment, and rural labor markets in Colombia, and taught Econometrics I there. I also worked at the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia, monitoring the money market and contributing to a text-mining project on households’ economic perceptions.
