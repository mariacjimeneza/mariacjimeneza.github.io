Bio

I grew up in Colombia, where I studied Economics at Jorge Tadeo Lozano University. My interest in gender and labour economics started there, working as a research assistant on projects on gender gaps in employment, including a study with the Inter-American Development Bank on women’s unemployment across Colombian cities. I then moved to France for an MSc in Economics at Aix-Marseille School of Economics, specialising in econometrics, and later worked as a data scientist at Colombia’s Financial Superintendence.

In 2022, I moved to L’Aquila to start my PhD at GSSI. During the PhD, I was a visiting researcher at Jönköping International Business School in Sweden and at the University of Liverpool. Spanish is my native language, and I am fluent in English. I also speak Italian and some French. Outside research, I enjoy sports, especially basketball and running, reading literature, and hiking in the mountains.
